Privacy policy

Updated: 2026-10-09

This policy describes data processing in MemeLab for Android. The developer and data contact is Pavlo Khomenko, hompavlik@gmail.com.

1. Data we process

  • Account: email, display name, Firebase user ID, email verification status and authentication information. Firebase Authentication handles your password; it is not stored in our profile database.
  • Generations: selected photos, an optional reference image, description, template, output, job timestamps and status. Photos are selected through the system file picker.
  • Service operation: job identifiers, quotas, safe error codes and technical logs.
  • Analytics and Crashlytics: usage events, screens, crash information, app and operating system versions, device model and installation identifiers. SDKs may also process network information for delivery and service protection.

2. Purposes

We use this data to provide sign-in and account recovery, generate requested images, store your personal collection, process deletion requests, limit abuse and fix errors. Usage statistics help identify useful features and problems.

We do not add names, email addresses, photos, prompts or account UIDs to Analytics events or custom Crashlytics fields. Advertising ID collection and advertising personalization are disabled. This version has no advertising SDKs.

3. Images of people

Face photos are used to generate the requested appearance. The app does not perform identity verification or maintain its own biometric template database to identify people. Upload your own photos or photos of people who have permitted this processing.

4. Service providers

Google Firebase provides authentication, database, file storage, backend processing, Analytics and Crashlytics. OpenAI API receives selected images and a description when you start generation; we do not include your profile name or email in that generation request. Providers may process data outside your country.

Under OpenAI API data controls, API content is not used for model training by default. Abuse monitoring logs may be retained for up to 30 days, subject to provider exceptions. Deleting MemeLab files does not immediately delete provider service records. See also Firebase privacy information.

5. Retention and deletion

Account, profile and output data remain until the corresponding image or account is deleted. Input photos and job descriptions are removed from MemeLab working storage and its queue after processing. Periodic cleanup targets abandoned uploads older than 24 hours; failures or a large backlog may delay cleanup.

Technical logs and SDK data follow the respective service settings and retention rules. Aggregate statistics may be retained separately. Deleting an account does not delete copies saved on your device or sent to other people.

6. Private collection

The app has no public feed. User access to your collection is restricted to your account; the developer and infrastructure providers process data to operate and support the service. Sharing an image through the system share menu sends it to the app or recipient you select.

7. Your requests

You can change your name in your profile. Delete data or your account in the app, or request deletion by email using the deletion instructions. You may also request access, corrections or information about processing at the same address. We may need to verify control of your account email. Never send your password.

If you believe a child's data has been provided without the necessary permission, contact the developer for review and deletion.

8. This website and changes

This policy website has no data collection forms, advertising or analytics scripts, or its own cookies. Firebase Hosting may process normal HTTP request information to serve and protect the site. We update this page and its date when the app changes.
