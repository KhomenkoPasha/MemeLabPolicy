Delete account

Updated: 2026-10-09

You can request deletion of your MemeLab account and associated data without installing the app. Developer: Pavlo Khomenko.

Request by email

  1. From your registered account email, write to hompavlik@gmail.com.
  2. Use the subject MemeLab — account deletion and state that you want your account and associated data deleted.
  3. If you no longer have access to that email, tell us so we can clarify how to verify account ownership.

Do not send passwords, identity documents or photos with your request. The email link opens your email app; this page does not submit a request automatically.

In the app

Open Profile → Delete account, confirm, and enter your password inside the app if requested. If deletion is unavailable or fails, use the email route above. Wait for an active generation to finish.

What is deleted

After account ownership is confirmed, deletion removes the Firebase account, profile, personal collection, input and output images, job queue and associated MemeLab service records. We will reply to confirm completion or explain any further steps needed.

You can also request deletion of individual images without deleting the account. Previously saved or shared copies remain on your device or with recipients. Technical logs, aggregate statistics and provider records follow the retention periods and exceptions described in the privacy policy.
